01 — About · Rólam

Modern cutting,
made personal.

I’m a freelance hairstylist working from a quiet studio at home — no waiting room, no background noise, just you and my full attention.

My philosophy on modern cutting is simple: the best haircut isn’t the loudest one. It follows the shape of your head and the way your hair naturally grows, it grows out gracefully, and it styles in minutes — so you look like yourself on a good day, every day.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We start with a conversation about your hair, your routine and what you want from the result.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Head shape, growth patterns and density guide every decision I make with the shears.

  3. 03

    Refine

    Texture, finish and a few honest styling tips for the morning after.

05 — Our Story · Miért készült ez az oldal?

Our Story.

The story behind the Beerbershop.

  1. September 9, 2025

    The First Haircut

    Mike promised Gyurka he would be his very first client. And so it happened — after a year of planning and learning, we “made the cut.” It turned out really well for a first try, though it definitely helped that on Mike’s blonde hair, the choppy fade transitions weren’t as visible.

  2. September 17, 2025

    The Planning

    Mike started designing the website and the logo just for fun. Later, on November 8, 2026, after moving to Budapest to continue his university studies, developing this website for Gyurka became a way to stay connected to his distant friend.

Memories

Baja — Budapest

The Friendship.

Where else could it have started but on the waterfront in Baja? Our parents often organized summer beach days, brought together by our brothers’ shared love for football. When Mike was born, we were probably already meeting on the beach. Obviously, our dads also had a great time at these gatherings, and that good mood rubbed off on us. The 40-degree sun, the scorching sand, and the cold water. All you need is a pint of beer, a football, a few friends, and you wish it would never end.

Among Mike’s very first childhood memories, Gyurka is in every single one. The earliest memory on the banks of the Sugovica: Gyurka warning Mike not to pop the bag of chips because the bottom would tear and spill all over the ground. Mike didn’t believe him. Gyurka was right.

Years passed, and the few pictures above cannot even show a fraction of the friendship we share. Today, there are hundreds of kilometers between Mike and Gyurka. The goodbye was hard, and although we didn’t plan for it, we finally shared a sad, tear-soaked farewell. The lack of our usual meetups inspired Mike to build this website, making him feel just a little bit closer to his dear friend. I hope you like it.
